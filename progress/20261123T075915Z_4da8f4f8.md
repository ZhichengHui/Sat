# 2026-11-23 — #141: main vs z3 on the suite and on harder SATLIB families (report only)

Session 4da8f4f8 (feature, via /work). No code, `bench/` or proof change. The full tables
and archive hashes are in the comment on #141.

## Method

- main 187a7b1 (release) against z3 5.4.1 `-dimacs`. Each instance is run by both solvers, one
  after the other, in alternating order. Limits: 60 s wall, 2 GiB `RLIMIT_AS`. Times are of the
  solver process only, without the harness.
- Instances are cut at the SATLIB `%` line, which avoids #111.
- Scripts and archives were kept in `/tmp` (outside the worktree) and deleted at the end.
- **z3 omits don't-care variables from its models**: 305 of them, up to 453 variables missing.
  `bench/check-model.py` rejects such models, so I checked them leniently (no clash, every clause
  satisfied). Our models were checked strictly. Anyone comparing against z3 models must allow this.

## Results

- **No wrong answer, crash or disagreement**: 2219 instances in 32 families plus the 102 of the
  suite.
- **Suite**: `bench/run.sh`, min of 3, gives 102/102 in 1.15 s (hole9 0.13 s). Solver time only,
  min of 3 per instance: ours 0.83 s, z3 3.18 s (hole9: 0.14 s vs 1.93 s).
- **Where we lose clearly**:
  - bmc: 58.8 s vs 6.1 s. bmc-galileo-8 takes 29 s vs 0.77 s; ibm-13 10.9 s vs 0.93 s.
  - hanoi5: 25.3 s vs 7.6 s.
  - Sub-second, 2–10×: ais, bw_large.d, par16, flat175/200.
  - uf/uuf150–200: 1.2–1.5×.
- **Where we win**:
  - hole10: 0.76 s vs 5.8 s.
  - uf/uuf225–250: uuf250 460 s vs 746 s.
- **Timeouts**: only par32 (10/10), for both solvers at 60 s.
- **Material for a graded harder suite** (owner's decision, `PLAN.md`):
  - bmc (0.02–29 s), hanoi4/5;
  - uf250 (0.04–8.8 s) and uuf250 (1.0–15 s) samples;
  - flat200, bw_large.c/d, hole10;
  - par32 as the unsolved end.

  More unsolved instances need sources outside SATLIB.
- **Lesson for tuning**: the structured instances (bmc, hanoi) are where we fall behind, and
  the present tuning on pigeon-hole and uuf100 does not cover them.
