- **Change** (`core/src/lib.rs`): the linked watch lists (`head`, `next`, `unlink`) are replaced
  by contiguous per-literal lists `watches: Vec<Vec<usize>>` of watch nodes. `propagate_lit` takes
  the list out with `mem::replace`, visits it oldest first and compacts it in place (MiniSat
  style); after a conflict the rest of the list is moved down by the new pure function
  `close_gap`. Two further cuts of the cost of a visit: `kill` sets the keys of both nodes of a
  dead clause to `NONE` (new `unwatch`), so a visit drops a dead clause on the already loaded
  other key and `is_dead` is gone; `false_lit: Vec<bool>` indexed by literal key (kept by `enqueue`
  and `backtrack`) makes `lit_true`/`lit_false`/`key_true` one load. Design of #45/#46 kept: the
  search stays untrusted, every index is guarded (`j < ws.len()` before `ws[j] = node`,
  `k < watches.len()` before the final store, since the state is unknown after `enqueue`).
