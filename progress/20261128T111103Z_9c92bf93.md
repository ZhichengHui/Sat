## Lessons

- **An experimental knob must not change the default path.** My first variant build saved and
  restored `inc` around `bump_reasons` to try a smaller reason increment. When `bump` rescaled
  inside it, the restore undid the rescale of `inc`, a rare silent activity reset. It made
  hanoi5 copies look 46 % better for every restart variant; without it the gain was 0–20 %. A
  check that caught it: the branch binary's output differed from the variant's on bmc-ibm-13.
  Compare the branch binary's output with the variant's before trusting a sweep.
- **The bmc gate on the 13 originals measures luck for any trajectory change.** The same
  candidate gave the bmc originals 1.02× the conflicts but +7 % in time, while on 52 fresh
  copies it was 0.95×. hanoi5 alone swings from −51 % to +51 % between neighbouring constants.
  A restart or decision change needs a gate on permuted copies, for example the geometric mean
  over 4 copies per instance.
- Lead: the accidental activity reset (inc not rescaled) helped hanoi5 a lot. A deliberate
  version (reset or strong rescaling of activities every N conflicts, or a faster decay only
  on instances like hanoi) is untested. Faster decay was worse on hole (#94).

## Follow-up (posted on #159)

- K = 0.9 with spacing 100 (patch below), or K = 1.0, with the bmc gate on permuted copies
  instead of the originals: the planner's or owner's call.

  minimisation have just read those literals. Collecting the level-0 variables they meet as the
