## Findings for the port

- A wrapped `len()` bounds the true length only under `rvec_wf`; the layout is part of the value. Hence the invariant in the `&mut self` contracts of the search. Reads need nothing new.
- `crush_base` and the post `∃g' s'. r ↦ g'↓s' ⋆ ⟨sok s'⟩`:
  - When the precondition gives the premise `sok s`, `crush_base` can close `sok ?s'` with it before the points-to is matched. It then leaves an unprovable `s = <new state>`. Seen in `watch`; not in `enqueue`.
  - Workaround: state the precondition as `sok_in s` with `sok_in ≡ sok`.
- AutoCorrode lacks a modify rule for `rvec_elems_focus`. The trial's `rvec_elems_focus_modify` is needed.
