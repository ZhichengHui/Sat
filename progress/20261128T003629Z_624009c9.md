## Change

- Candidate (a) of the issue. `Search` gains `hint0: Vec<usize>`, the variables of level 0 the
  conflict needs. Each is listed after those its own reason needs: a post-order DFS over level-0
  reasons on `stack`, marking with `seen` and unmarking afterwards. The roots are the conflict
  clause and the reasons of `hint`.
  - The DFS methods are `frame0`, `step0`, `close0`, `close_hint`, `unmark0` and `level0_hint`.
  - `certify` forces it with `force_fix(hint0, 0)`, the existing fixpoint pass, before the other
    hints. A wrong order only costs a second pass, and a wrong hint only fails the check.
- **Adaptive**: `level0_hint` builds `hint0` only if `lim[0] > hint.len()`, and returns `lo = 0`;
  otherwise it returns `lim[0]`, and `certify` re-forces the prefix as before.
  - On uuf100/hole the prefix is about one variable (once a unit is learned). There, the
    always-on DFS rescanned every hinted reason for nothing: +3.3 % (A), +3.1 % (hole8) and
    +2.9 % (hole9) in a first guard run, which failed the gate.
  - With the rule, hole9 copies are +0.058 % in instructions (about 50 per conflict: the call
    and an empty `force_fix`), and time is within noise.
- Trajectory unchanged: byte-identical output to main on all 15 bmc/hanoi instances. `cdcl` never
  gave up on the 208 permuted copies (harness answers, no `None`).
- Proofs:
  - `sok` gains `vok L8 (Search_hint0 s)` (as #116 did for `hint`).
  - New theory `proof/Sat_Level0.thy` (between `Sat_Reduce` and `Sat_CDCL`; 17 s): no-panic plus
    `sok` contracts. Its method `sok0` is `sok_fast` with a `sok_hint0` rule.
  - `Sat_CDCL`: `on_conflict` uses `Search_level0_hint_spec`. `certify`'s proof is unchanged: its
    `force_fix_spec` covers the second call. Its contract is unchanged.
  - Locale and `spec/` unchanged.
- Not done: (b), resetting only the touched marks. After (a), the memset is 5.3 % on galileo-9 (vs
  `close0` 4.3 %). Clearing from an untrusted list cannot be checked cheaply, so it needs an
  invariant on `marks` across calls, which the checked design avoids. It is a lead only with such
  an invariant in `sok`.

## Lessons / leads

- **An untrusted hint must not cost more than the check it replaces.** The level-0 DFS paid off
  only where the prefix is long (bmc: 3k–27k) and lost 3 % where it is about 1 (hole). The rule
  `lim[0] > hint.len()` compares the two costs without a tuned constant.
- After (a), the largest parts on galileo-9 are `cdcl`'s propagation (71 %), `backtrack` (6 %), the
  memset of `cleared` (5.3 %) and `close0` (4.3 %).
- An `apply` that names the loop's reference (`subgoal for i_ref`) failed when the function had an
  early `return` before the loop: the goal has other parameters. Put the loop in its own function
  (`close_hint`), and keep early returns in loop-free functions.

  (outside the worktree) and deleted at the end.
