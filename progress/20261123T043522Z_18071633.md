## Note: when the guard reads `lbd_sum`/`nconf`

`analyze` does not update the counters itself. `on_conflict` computes the LBD of the new clause
(`learnt_lbd`) after `analyze` returns, and `cdcl` adds it to `lbd_sum`/`nconf` in `restart_due`
after `on_conflict`. So the guard reads the average over the conflicts *before* the current one.
After many conflicts this one-conflict lag does not matter. Early it does:
- At the first conflict `lbd_sum = nconf = 0` and `0 >= 0` holds, so the first conflict always
  bumps reasons.
- The early averages over a handful of conflicts are noisy.

This fits the uuf100 residual of #132 ("early conflicts can have an average LBD above 6.5"). It is
a lead for #134 (e.g. require `nconf > 0`, or include the current LBD), not changed here.
