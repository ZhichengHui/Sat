# 2026-11-19 UTC — feature session 8468478b (via /work), issue #73

- **Change** (`core/src/lib.rs`): recursive learned-clause minimisation (MiniSat's `litRedundant`,
  Sörensson–Biere 2009) instead of the local one. `lit_redundant(v)` runs a depth-first search
  over the reasons on an explicit stack `stack: Vec<usize>` of three-word frames
  `(v, next arena position, end of the reason's entry)`; it reads the reasons' literal keys from
  the clause arena of #71 (not through `lit_at`). A variable is fine if it is in the clause
  (`seen`), of level 0, or found redundant before (marked `seen` when pushed); the search fails at
  a decision, a variable known not redundant (`fail`), or one whose level no literal of the clause
  has (exact level filter: `minimize` first calls `learnt_lbd`, which stamps the clause's levels
  with `tick`). On failure `unwind` marks the frames on the stack (the path) not redundant and
  unmarks them; `clear_marks` clears `seen`/`fail` of every variable in `toclear` after the
  conflict. One step of the search is its own function (`redundant_step`, `push_frame` last), so
  the loop of `lit_redundant` only calls it. New fields `fail`, `stack`, `toclear` of existing types:
  no locale or template change. Design of #45/#46 kept: the search stays untrusted, `certify` still
  checks every learned clause, every index is guarded.
