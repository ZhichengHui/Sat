## Change (deliverable 2)

`analyze` bumps reasons while `fast ≥ 0x68000` (6.5 · 2^16): the fast moving average of
`restart_due` (weight 1/32) instead of `lbd_sum / nconf`, the same threshold. `fast` starts at 0,
so early long clauses of random 3-SAT no longer switch bumping on (uuf100 0.4 % of conflicts).
No new field; the guard has no arithmetic, so `analyze_guard_bound` and its step in
`Search_analyze_spec` are gone. Doc comment of `bump_reasons` updated.

Negative results (tuning seeds 9801–9816):
- bumping each reason variable at most once per conflict is not possible cheaply; skipping the
  variables of the learned clause (already bumped by `analyze_clause`) and level 0: hole9 conflicts
  ×2.05, hole8 ×1.58 — the double bump of the learned clause's variables is what pays.
- `fast` thresholds 5.0–7.5: within noise of 6.5 on hole, 5.0/5.5 give uuf100 more conflicts;
  kept 6.5 (no retuning).
- Early LBDs overlap (first 50 conflicts: hole7 6–7.5, uuf100 5–7; hole8/9 7.5–11), so no LBD
  signal keeps hole7's early bumping and drops uuf100's: hole7 pays ≈ +7–11 % conflicts.
